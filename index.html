    How to play

    • Goal: be the last player who has not exploded.
    • Your turn: play any cards you like, then draw one to end your turn.
    • Exploding Kitten: play a Defuse and hide it back in the deck, or you are out.
    • Nope cancels any action, even on someone else's turn. Tap it when it glows.
    • Cat cards: a pair steals a random card; three of a kind names the card you want.
